Math Projects

Rubric for Math Project.

The items below count for 5 points each, a total of 100 points.

  1. Title page
  2. Statement of task
  3. How you will do it
  4. Hypothesis
  5. Describe how you collected data and the subject people
  6. Add you survey questions and the quantity of data
  7. 7–13) Graphs: Two Frequency tables, two Box and Whiskers, and a Scatter plot
  8. Include the raw data
  9. Subtitle the process: (Eg.) 1. Introduction, 2. Data, 3. Graph, 4. Interpretation, 5. Conclusion
  10. Use formula & show Calculation
  11. Scatter plot with interpretation
  12. Interpret every math process and explain their meaning.
  13. If results are inconclusive or contradictory, discuss possible reasons.
  14. Discuss what you might do differently, if you were to repeat this project.
