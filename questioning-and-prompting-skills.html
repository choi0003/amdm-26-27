Questioning and Prompting Skills

Mathematical Short Story Questioning Templates and Critical Thinking Prompts

What is Prompt Engineering?

  • Definition: Giving clear and complete instructions to an AI to get useful output.
  • Importance: The quality of the prompt determines the quality of the AI's response.

Parts of a Good Math Prompt

  • Context (e.g., "A car travels 50 miles...")
  • Question (e.g., "How long will it take at 60 mph?")
  • Intent (e.g., "Show step-by-step solution.")
  • Constraints (e.g., "Assume constant speed. Use only algebra.")

Examples of AI-Friendly Prompts

Example 1: English / Literature Analysis (Focus: Comparative Analysis)

Poor: "Summarize Romeo and Juliet."

Better: "Explain the theme of fate versus free will in Shakespeare's Romeo and Juliet with examples."

Best: "Act as an expert English teacher. Analyze how Shakespeare uses light and dark imagery to contrast fate and free will in Act 2, Scene 2 of Romeo and Juliet. Instead of giving me a summary, guide me through 3 key quotes with questions that help me analyze the symbolism myself."

Why it works: It turns the AI into an interactive tutor that prompts critical thinking rather than writing the essay for the student.

Example 2: Science / Biology (Focus: Conceptual Understanding & Metaphor)

Poor: "What is photosynthesis?"

Better: "Explain the steps of photosynthesis in detail."

Best: "Explain the process of photosynthesis using a real-world analogy (like a factory or a solar-powered kitchen) so a high school student can easily understand it. Break it down into light-dependent and light-independent reactions, and explain why plants produce oxygen as a byproduct."

Why it works: It asks the AI to reframe complex scientific concepts into intuitive, easy-to-grasp mental models.

Example 3: History / Social Studies (Focus: Multi-Perspective Thinking)

Poor: "Why did World War I start?"

Better: "List the main causes of World War I and explain the M-A-I-N acronym."

Best: "Compare the immediate economic and political impacts of the Treaty of Versailles from two contrasting perspectives: a German citizen in 1919 and a French diplomat in 1919. Present the response as a side-by-side comparison with a brief reflection on how these views set the stage for future conflict."

Why it works: It pushes the AI to generate nuanced, historical empathy and multi-faceted analysis rather than a simple facts list.

Math Essay: Take a sample essay writing from Percentage Error Essay.

For the first paragraph;

Poor: "What is percentage error?"

Better: "Define percentage error and show the formula."

Best: "Act as a math teacher. Explain the concept of 'Percentage Error' for a high school math paper. Include its formal definition, why it is used in real life, and its mathematical formula ($Va$ and $Ve$). Keep it concise and formatted as an introductory paragraph."

For the second paragraph;

Poor: "Give me an example of percentage error."

Better: "Give me a real-life example of percentage error with numbers and solve it."

Best: "Create a realistic scenario using percentage error (such as estimating concert attendance vs. actual turnout). Show the step-by-step calculation clearly, explicitly explaining how the absolute value bar applies when subtracting $Va$ from $Ve$. Write this as a second paragraph continuing from the definition above."

Best Prompt for both of the two paragraphs

"Act as a math writing assistant. Write a two-paragraph explanation of 'Percentage Error' suitable for an AMDM assignment:

  • Paragraph 1: Define Percentage Error, explain why we use it, and state the formula using $Va$ (approximated) and $Ve$ (exact).
  • Paragraph 2: Provide a practical real-life example (e.g., estimating 70 attendees vs. 80 actual attendees). Show the complete calculation step-by-step, including an explanation of how the absolute value works in the formula."*

Template 1: Mathematical Concept Identification

Initial Reading Questions

  • What's happening in this story? Summarize the main events in 2-3 sentences.
  • What numbers, measurements, or patterns do you notice? List at least 3 mathematical elements.
  • What mathematical concept(s) might be hidden in this story? (Examples: probability, statistics, functions, geometry, etc.)

Deeper Analysis Prompts

  • How does the mathematical concept drive the story forward?
  • What would happen to the story if the mathematical element was different?
  • Can you identify the mathematical problem the characters are trying to solve?

Template 2: Connection Making Framework

Personal Connections

  • Have you ever encountered a similar mathematical situation in real life? Describe it.
  • How does this story relate to what we've learned in class recently?
  • What other mathematical topics does this story remind you of?

Mathematical Connections

  • What mathematical tools or formulas could help solve the problem in this story?
  • How does this concept connect to other math topics we've studied?
  • Where else might you see this mathematical concept used in the real world?

Story-to-Concept Bridges

  • If you had to teach this mathematical concept using this story, what would be your main points?
  • What mathematical vocabulary from our unit appears in or relates to this story?

Template 3: Student Inquiry Generation

"What if..." Questions

  • What if the numbers in the story were different?
  • What if the characters made different mathematical choices?
  • What if we applied this concept to a different situation?

"How might..." Questions

  • How might the characters use mathematics to improve their situation?
  • How might this mathematical concept help solve other problems?
  • How might we test the mathematical accuracy of what happened in the story?

"Why do you think..." Questions

  • Why do you think the author chose these specific numbers/measurements?
  • Why do you think this mathematical concept is important to understand?
  • Why do you think the mathematical solution worked (or didn't work)?

Advanced Inquiry Prompts

  • Create a mathematical investigation: What could we research or calculate based on this story?
  • Design a follow-up problem: What mathematical question could extend this story?
  • Challenge the story: Is the mathematics in this story realistic? How could you verify it?

Template 4: Critical Thinking Progression

Level 1: Understanding (Basic)

  • What mathematical concept is demonstrated in this story?
  • What are the key mathematical facts presented?
  • Can you restate the mathematical problem in your own words?

Level 2: Analyzing (Intermediate)

  • How does the mathematical concept influence the characters' decisions?
  • What assumptions are the characters making about the mathematical situation?
  • What evidence supports or contradicts the mathematical reasoning in the story?

Level 3: Evaluating (Advanced)

  • How effective was the mathematical approach used by the characters?
  • What alternative mathematical strategies could have been used?
  • How reliable is the mathematical information presented in the story?

Level 4: Creating (Expert)

  • How would you modify this story to demonstrate a different mathematical concept?
  • What new mathematical questions does this story inspire you to investigate?
  • How could you use this story to teach someone else about this mathematical concept?

Template 5: Reflection and Application

Mathematical Reflection Questions

  • What did this story help you understand about mathematics that you didn't know before?
  • How has your thinking about this mathematical concept changed after reading the story?
  • What mathematical skills did you use while analyzing this story?

Application Planning

  • Where could you apply this mathematical concept in your own life?
  • How might professionals use this mathematical concept in their work?
  • What mathematical tools would help you solve similar problems?

Future Learning Goals

  • What mathematical questions do you still have after reading this story?
  • What related mathematical topics would you like to explore further?
  • How could you use storytelling to help others understand mathematical concepts?
