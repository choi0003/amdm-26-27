Rubrics with Sample Writing

Assessments

Multiple Formatives (40%):

  1. Creating a prompt (60% of Formative grade)
    Create a prompt regarding the lesson for the day and try to put a reason why you came up with it.
  2. A Paragraph Writing (80% of Formative grade)
    In a paragraph, you will write the following items, using the internet.
    • What is “(your chosen topic)”? and its definition.
    • Explain the topic; The Formula, the content's history and so on.
  3. Two-Paragraph Writing (100% of Formative grade)
    In addition to the first paragraph, you will add a paragraph that includes the following components
    • What is a real-life example of the topic?
    • You will continue to show and explain the real-life example with "calculations"
    • Use the mathematical process (or formula) and show how it applies to the real-life example.
  4. Math Notebook Check (100% of Formative grade)
    Students will have their math notebooks checked frequently for assessment. The notebook checking is based on the rubric, and the rubric is displayed on the classroom board for reference.
  5. Quiz (70% of Formative grade)
    Students will take a short quiz (2~3 problems) to understand the contents

Summative (50%):

  1. One-page essay (100% of summative grade)
    "Building on the two paragraphs above, you will add the following items to create a reflective conclusion."
    • What did you learn from the real-life example?
    • Why is it important and meaningful?
    • Where else can you use it in real life?
    • How does it help you to make better decisions? And you will try to mathematically justify it
    • Also include any importance, implications, or suggestions of the topic.
